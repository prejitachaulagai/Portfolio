About (Me)

I'm a graphic designer working out of Kathmandu, currently at Hilife Foods, handling branding and packaging for a growing food company. Before that: a year and a half at Ecstatic Minds, and my start as an intern at Majestic Marketing.

My path here is a little unusual — I hold a First Class Honours degree in Computer Science from Herald College Kathmandu, affiliated with the University of Wolverhampton, UK. It's not the typical design-school route, but it shapes how I work: structured, detail-driven, and comfortable with technical constraints.

Day to day, I'm focused on branding, packaging, and print — building visual identities that hold up from a business card to a billboard.

Thanks for taking the time to visit my portfolio!
